About me

I am a Ph.D. student in Communication Studies at Michigan State University.

My research examines how human–AI interaction shapes self-identity and interpersonal relationships. I am particularly interested in how language and social cues in human–AI conversations shape people’s perceptions, judgments, and responses.

I use experimental and computational methods to study these questions. You can explore my research and meet my collaborators here.
